- consultants, auditors and penetration testers who run Benchmark on a client's environment, or deliver its reports to clients;
- managed service providers (MSPs and MSSPs) who run it for their customers;
- offering Benchmark, or its results, as a hosted or managed service;
- reselling, rebranding, bundling or redistributing Benchmark, or building another product on it.

## How to get one

Contact Kod-Dot through [GitHub](https://github.com/Kod-Dot) with your organization's name, how you plan to use Benchmark and roughly how many clients or environments you will assess. Licences are offered per consultant or per organization.
